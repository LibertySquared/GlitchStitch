Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the user's two attached references. Transparent PNG masters are embedded in the app. `../glitchstich.ico` packages the app design at 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 96, 128 and 256 pixels; `tools/Build-BrandAssets.ps1` rebuilds that Windows container without changing the artwork.

## App icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished square desktop application icon for GlitchStich, a cyberpunk video editor. Reference 1 supplies the cheerful cartoon thumbs-up pose, bold readable silhouette and friendly mascot energy; reference 2 supplies the twin-reel movie-camera head, glowing camera lens and dark cyan/magenta cyberpunk materials. Reinterpret both into an original cohesive stylized 3D cartoon mascot: compact camera-headed upper-body character giving one large clear thumbs-up, dark futuristic jacket, cyan lens with a magenta highlight, two clearly readable film reels above the head. Friendly and inviting, not threatening. Three-quarter view facing the viewer. Strong simplified shapes and clean edges, carefully readable at 32 and 48 pixels; avoid tiny circuitry, city scenery, rain and photographic clutter. A restrained dark badge with a luminous cyan/magenta rim may frame the mascot, with a transparent exterior. Center the complete character with generous safe margins, no cropped thumb or reels. No lettering, no signage, no watermark. True transparent background outside the icon silhouette. Deliver only this one icon, no contact sheet or presentation mockup.
